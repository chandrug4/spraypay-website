Who We Are

Redefining Fragrance Access

Spray-Pay was born from a simple belief — luxury fragrance should be accessible to everyone, everywhere.

We design and operate smart, self-service fragrance vending machines placed at premium venues. Our machines feature physical scent-test buttons, an intuitive quantity selector, and seamless UPI payments — making premium perfume available 24/7 without counters or salespeople.

Starting from Pondicherry, we're building India's first network of automated fragrance kiosks for malls, hotels, offices, and events. Every spray is precision-dosed, contactless, and affordable — luxury on your terms.

The Experience

How Spray-Pay Works

01

Discover

Walk up to the machine and explore our curated selection of premium fragrances. Each scent has a physical test button — press it to sample.

02

Select & Customise

Choose your number of sprays with the intuitive touchscreen. Watch the animated bottle fill up as you dial in the perfect amount.

03

Pay via UPI

Scan the generated QR code with any UPI app — PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, or your bank app. Instant, secure, contactless payment.

04

Spray

Your premium fragrance is dispensed automatically with precision timing. Enjoy a lasting, all-day scent in seconds.
